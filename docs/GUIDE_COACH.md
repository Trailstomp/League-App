# 🏃 Coach / Team Admin User Guide

## Your Role
As a **Coach** or **Team Admin**, you manage your team's roster, events, and settings. You're the go-to person for your players and the link between them and the league.

---

## What You Can Do

✅ Manage your team's roster (add/remove players)
✅ Edit player positions and jersey numbers
✅ Create team-specific events (practices, meetings)
✅ Score your team's games (live scoring)
✅ Manage team settings (social media, colors)
✅ View your players' stats and attendance
✅ Send team communications
✅ Access team documents via File Manager
✅ View recruiting applications for your team

## What Requires League Admin

❌ Create games (requires league coordination)
❌ Edit other teams' information
❌ Modify league-wide settings
❌ Access other teams' private data

---

## Getting Started

### Access Your Team Page
1. Log in to the portal
2. Click your team name in the sidebar
3. Or set your team page as your default landing page (click the ⭐ star)

### Team Page Tabs
| Tab | What's There |
|-----|--------------|
| **Overview** | Team summary, upcoming events |
| **Roster** | Player cards, add/edit/remove players |
| **Stats** | Team and individual statistics |
| **Schedule** | Team's events calendar |
| **Gallery** | Team photos and media |
| **Settings** | YouTube, social media, colors |

## Managing Your Roster

### Adding a New Player
1. Go to your team's **Roster** tab
2. Click **"+ Add Player"** button (top right)
3. Search for the player by name or email
4. Select the player from results
5. The player is now on your roster!

### Editing Player Details
1. Hover over a player card
2. Click the **pencil icon** (edit)
3. Update:
   - Jersey Number
   - Position (Attack, Midfield, Defense, Goalie, FOGO, LSM)
4. Click **Save Changes**

### Removing a Player
1. Hover over the player card
2. Click the **trash icon** (remove)
3. Confirm removal
4. Player is removed from YOUR team only (they may still be on other teams)

### Player Cards
Each player card shows:
- Player photo (or placeholder)
- Name and position
- Jersey number
- Team logo
- Click card to see full player profile

## Team Events

### Viewing Your Schedule
1. Go to team **Schedule** tab
2. See all upcoming events
3. Filter by event type if needed

### Creating a Practice
1. Go to Schedule tab
2. Click **"Add Event"**
3. Select Type: **Practice**
4. Set date, time, location
5. Enable RSVP to track attendance
6. Save

### Creating a Team Meeting
1. Add Event → Type: **Meeting**
2. Can be in-person or virtual (add video link)
3. Enable RSVP for headcount

### Tracking RSVPs
1. Open any event
2. View RSVP responses:
   - ✅ Going
   - ❌ Not Going
   - ❓ No Response
3. Send reminders to those who haven't responded

## Live Game Scoring

### Before the Game
1. Find your game on the Schedule
2. Click **"Live Score"** to open scoring interface
3. Verify your roster is loaded correctly
4. Check opponent roster is visible

### Scoring a Goal
1. Click **"Goal"** button under scoring team
2. Optionally select the player who scored
3. Score updates immediately

### Recording Saves
1. When goalie makes a save
2. Click **"Save"** button
3. Optionally assign to specific goalie

### Recording Misses
1. For shots that miss the goal
2. Click **"Miss"** button
3. Tracks shot attempts for accuracy stats

### Shot Clock
- Use toggle to enable/disable shot clock
- Clock resets after goals and possessions
- Audio alert when time expires

### Ending the Game
1. Verify final score is correct
2. Review player stats
3. Click **"Mark as Final"**
4. Game appears in ticker with final score

## Team Settings

### Social Media Links
1. Go to **Settings** tab
2. Click **"Social Media"** section
3. Enter your handles:
   - Instagram: @yourteam
   - Twitter: @yourteam
   - Facebook: facebook.com/yourteam
   - TikTok: @yourteam
   - YouTube: channel link
   - Website: team website
4. Click **Save Social Media**

### Team Appearance
1. Settings → **Appearance** section
2. Customize:
   - **Primary Color** - Main team color
   - **Accent Color** - Secondary color
   - **Logo URL** - Team logo image
   - **Banner URL** - Header banner image
3. Preview shows how it will look
4. Click **Save Appearance**

### YouTube Channel
1. Settings → **YouTube Channel** section
2. Enter YouTube channel ID or URL
3. Videos will display on your team page

## Communicating with Your Team

### Player Invitations
1. Go to **Recruiting** tab (if available)
2. Enter new player's info
3. Choose email or SMS
4. Send invitation
5. Player receives link to join

### Email Composer
If league admin has granted access:
1. Go to Email section
2. Select your team's players as recipients
3. Compose and send messages
4. Team-level SMTP can be configured for your team

### Event Reminders
- Automatic reminders based on RSVP settings
- Manual: Open event → "Send Reminder"

### Team Documents
1. Access your team's file manager
2. Upload game plans, schedules, or waivers
3. Use the league's cloud connection or your own storage

## Common Questions

### "How do I add someone not in the system?"
Contact your League Admin to create the user account first, then add them to your roster.

### "Can a player be on two teams?"
Yes! Players can be on multiple teams. Just add them to your roster - they keep their other team assignments.

### "How do I change someone's role?"
Role changes (player to coach) require Admin access. Contact your League Admin.

### "My player's photo isn't showing"
- Check URL is a direct image link
- For Google Drive: ensure sharing is set to "Anyone with link"
- Try a different image host if issues persist

### "Game score is wrong"
If game is still "In Progress" - edit directly in live scoring.
If game is "Final" - contact League Admin to unlock and correct.
