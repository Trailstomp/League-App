# 👀 Guest / Visitor User Guide

## Welcome!
As a **Guest**, you can browse public information about the league, teams, and events without logging in. This guide shows you what's available.

---

## What You Can See

✅ League homepage and branding
✅ Team listings and basic info
✅ Public event schedule
✅ Live ticker with game scores
✅ Team rosters (public view)
✅ Photo galleries (public ones)
✅ League standings

## What Requires an Account

❌ RSVP to events
❌ View detailed player stats
❌ Access team-specific content
❌ Participate in live scoring
❌ Download player cards

---

## Navigating the Site

### Homepage
When you first visit, you'll see:
- **Event Ticker** - Scrolling display of games and events
- **Team Cards** - Quick view of all teams
- **Division Navigation** - Teams organized by division
- **League Announcements** - News and updates

### The Ticker
The ticker at the top shows:
- Recent game scores (e.g., "Eagles 3 - Hawks 1")
- Upcoming events
- Tournaments and special events

**Tip:** Hover over the ticker to pause it!

---

## Viewing Teams

### Finding a Team
1. Look in the sidebar under "TEAMS"
2. Teams are grouped by division
3. Click division name to expand/collapse
4. Click team name to view

### Team Information
On each team's public page:
- Team logo and colors
- Current record
- Roster (player names and positions)
- Upcoming schedule
- Photo gallery

### Reading Team Records
Example: **Lions (5-2)**
- 5 wins, 2 losses
- Records update after each game

---

## Viewing the Schedule

### On Homepage
The ticker shows upcoming events automatically

### Team Schedules
1. Click on a team
2. Look for Schedule or Events tab
3. See upcoming games and practices

### Event Types
| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| UPCOMING | Future scheduled event |
| LIVE | Currently in progress |
| FINAL | Completed with score |
| CANCELLED | Event was cancelled |

---

## Viewing Rosters

### Team Roster View
1. Go to a team page
2. Click "Roster" tab
3. See player cards

### Player Cards Show
- Player photo (if available)
- Name
- Position
- Jersey number
- Team affiliation

---

## Understanding Scores

### In the Ticker
Scored games show:
```
Eagles 3 - Hawks 1
Oct 20
```
- Home team listed first
- Final score
- Game date

### Live Games
If a game is live:
- Shows "LIVE" badge (pulsing)
- Score updates in real-time
- Time/period if available

---

## Photo Galleries

### Public Galleries
Some galleries are public:
1. Look for "Gallery" tabs on team pages
2. Browse photos by event or date
3. Click to enlarge images

### Gallery Types
- **Team Galleries** - Team-specific photos
- **Event Galleries** - Photos from specific events
- **League Galleries** - League-wide content

---

## Standings

### Viewing Standings
1. Look for "Standings" link
2. Shows teams ranked by record
3. Organized by division

### Standings Include
- Win/Loss record
- Win percentage
- Points (if applicable)
- Division rank

---

## Want to Join?

### Creating an Account
If you want to join the league:
1. Click "Register" or "Sign Up"
2. Fill in your information
3. Account may require admin approval
4. Once approved, log in to access more features

### Contact the League
Look for:
- Contact information on homepage
- Admin email
- Social media links

---

## Mobile Viewing

### On Your Phone
The site works on mobile devices:
- Sidebar becomes a menu (tap ☰)
- Ticker scrolls horizontally
- Cards stack vertically
- All info is accessible

### Tips for Mobile
- Rotate to landscape for schedule views
- Pinch to zoom on photos
- Tap ticker to interact with events

## Frequently Asked Questions

### "How do I see game results?"
Look at the ticker - scored games show final scores with team names.

### "When is the next game?"
Check the ticker for upcoming events, or click on a team to see their schedule.

### "How do I find a specific player?"
Go to their team's Roster tab and look through the player cards.

### "Can I get score notifications?"
Not as a guest. Create an account to access notification features.

### "Who runs this league?"
Check the homepage footer or "About" section for league information.

### "How do I register my child?"
Look for registration links, or contact the league admin through the site.
