# 🥍 Player User Guide

## Your Role
As a **Player**, you can view your stats, see your team's schedule, respond to events, and manage your profile. This is your personal hub for everything related to your lacrosse experience.

---

## What You Can Do

✅ View your player profile and stats
✅ See your team roster and teammates
✅ View upcoming events and games
✅ RSVP to events (going/not going)
✅ See your career statistics by year
✅ Download your player card
✅ View team galleries and photos

## What Requires Coach Access

❌ Edit other players' information
❌ Add/remove players from roster
❌ Create or modify events
❌ Access team admin settings

---

## Getting Started

### Logging In
1. Go to your league's portal URL
2. Click **"Login"** in the sidebar
3. **Sign in with Google** (one-click) or enter your email and password
4. Click **"Sign In"**

### First Time Login
If you received a password reset link:
1. Click the link in your email
2. Create a new password
3. Log in with your new password

If you signed up with Google, you can set a password later in Account Settings.

### Install the App on Your Phone
The portal is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**:
1. Visit the league portal in your mobile browser
2. Tap the browser menu (three dots)
3. Select **"Add to Home Screen"** (Android) or **"Add to Home Screen"** (iOS Safari share menu)
4. The app icon will appear on your home screen with the league logo
5. Opens full-screen like a native app

### Setting Your Default Page
1. Navigate to your team's page
2. Click the **star icon** on your preferred tab
3. Next time you log in, you'll land there automatically

## Your Player Profile

### Finding Your Card
1. Click your team in the sidebar
2. Go to the **Roster** tab
3. Find your player card
4. Click it to see full details

### Your Player Card Shows
**Front:**
- Your photo (or placeholder)
- Jersey number
- Position
- Team logo and colors

**Back:**
- Season statistics (Goals, Assists, Points)
- Career stats by year
- Lacrosse history (HS, College, Post-Grad)
- Fun facts

### Downloading Your Card
1. Click your player card
2. Click **"📥 Save PDF"**
3. Two-page PDF downloads with front and back

### Printing Your Card
1. Click your player card
2. Click **"🖨️ Print"**
3. Print preview opens
4. Print on card stock for best results

## Events & Schedule

### Viewing Upcoming Events
1. Check the **ticker** at the top (shows next games)
   - On mobile: compact ESPN-style strip below the header
   - On desktop: full scrolling ticker with detailed scores
2. Or go to your team's **Schedule** tab
3. See all practices, games, meetings

### Viewing Tournaments
1. Find tournament events in the schedule
2. Click to open the **Tournament Bracket**
3. View the horizontal bracket layout with round progression
4. Connector lines show matchup paths
5. Champion is highlighted with a trophy badge
6. Toggle between Bracket View and Games List

### Understanding Event Types
| Icon | Type | What It Is |
|------|------|------------|
| 🟢 | Game | Match against another team |
| 🟣 | Tournament | Multi-game competition |
| 🔵 | Practice | Team training session |
| 🟡 | Meeting | Team or league meeting |
| 🩷 | Social | Team social event |

### Reading the Ticker
The scrolling ticker shows:
- **Scored games** - Shows final score (3-1, 2-0, etc.)
- **Upcoming games** - Shows date and teams
- **Tournaments** - Shows tournament badge and details
- **Events** - Shows date and title
- Hover to pause scrolling (desktop)
- Mobile ticker auto-scrolls horizontally in a compact strip

## RSVPing to Events

### From Event Card
1. Find the event
2. Click the event to open details
3. Select your response:
   - **✅ Going** - You'll be there
   - **❌ Not Going** - Can't make it
4. Your response is saved automatically

### From Email/SMS Link
1. Click the RSVP link in message
2. Select your response
3. Confirmation page appears

### Changing Your Response
1. Open the event again
2. Select your new response
3. It updates automatically

### Why RSVP Matters
- Coaches use RSVPs to plan
- Helps with field reservations
- Ensures enough players for games
- Shows commitment to team

## Common Questions

### "How do I change my password?"
1. Click **"Account Settings"** in the sidebar
2. Enter your new password
3. Save

### "How do I update my email?"
Contact your coach or league admin to update your account email.

### "My stats seem wrong"
Talk to your coach - they can review and correct game entries.

### "I'm on two teams - which shows?"
You can switch between teams in the sidebar. Your stats are tracked separately per team.

### "I can't see some events"
Some events may be team-specific or require RSVP access. Check with your coach.

### "How do I install the app on my phone?"
Visit the site in your mobile browser, tap the menu, and select "Add to Home Screen". It installs with the league logo as the app icon.
